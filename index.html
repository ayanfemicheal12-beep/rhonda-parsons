The Author

A voice that lives
between the lines

Rhonda Parsons is a poet who thrives in community — sharing stages with fellow poets and bringing words to life in spaces where they resonate. She has performed for Art Scene's Prelude to Poetry and is a regular contributor to Poetically Yours on WNIJ Radio.

Her poetry is born from life lived honestly — from storms weathered and stillness found. With Take Five, she invites readers and listeners into a space of brave reflection, where resilience isn't just survival, but a full blooming.
