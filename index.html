About the Author

A Voice That Lives
Between the Lines

Rhonda Parsons is a poet who thrives in community — sharing stages with fellow poets and bringing words to life in spaces where they resonate most. She has performed for Art Scene's Prelude to Poetry and is a proud, regular contributor to Poetically Yours on WNIJ Radio.

Her poetry is born from life lived honestly — from storms weathered, stillness discovered, and the quiet strength found in prayer, meditation, and love. Through her debut collection Take Five, Rhonda invites readers and listeners into a space of brave reflection, where resilience isn't just survival — it's a full, beautiful blooming.

Beyond the page, Rhonda loves animals, crocheting, and walking outdoors in nature — the same nature that breathes through every verse she writes.
